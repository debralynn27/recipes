Stuffed Cabbage Recipe

Ingredients


  • Head of cabbage
  • Large pot of water
  • 1.5 lb ground meat
  • 0.5lb ground pork
  • 2 eggs
  • ~1 cup minute rice or 1/2 cup basmati rice
  • Salt and pepper
  • ~2tbs water
  • 2 cans tomato soup
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • Sauerkraut
  • Boil cabbage until leaves are softened
  • Remove head from water, peal off to leaves and place back in water
  • Cut off heel (thick party of cabbage leaf) so that leaves roll easier
  • Keep an eye on the cabbage, as leaves soften remove from head and replace cabbage on water. Cut off heel from cabbage leaves.
  • Combine ground beef and pork. Add 2 eggs. Add some salt and pepper, ~1/2 cup of rice, and a bit a 2tbs water.
  • Stuff cabbage leaves with meat mixture and roll. Fold flap over meat and start rolling, then tuck in the sides and continuing rolling tightly.
  • Remove rest of cabbage head from water.
  • Save some of the cabbage broth and dump out the rest.
  • Cut remaining cabbage head in half and cut out hard stem. Dice cabbage into empty pot.
  • Add can of tomato soup and about a half a can of broth. Layer larger rolls on the bottom and top with more cut up cabbage and a little bit of drained sauerkraut. Continue layering.
  • Bring to a boil uncovered
